Information in Logic — explainers

Three scroll-driven, self-contained explainers dramatizing mechanisms from the paper. Scroll to scrub through each one; scrolling back up reverses it smoothly.

  • Less is More

    Alice knows exactly what she wants Bob to end up able to prove. A short, pre-agreed list of candidate kernels lets her send less than either obvious strategy — while Bob ends up able to prove more than was strictly required.

  • No Need to Know

    Alice doesn't know what Bob already knows. She hashes her kernel into a short index; Bob rules out same-bin "confounders" using his own background knowledge.

  • The Price of Incorrect Information

    Alice and Bob packed for a trip using forecasts checked at different times. Being merely outdated costs little to fix — but a confidently wrong forecast costs far more, and the gap only grows as Bob gets more sure of it.
